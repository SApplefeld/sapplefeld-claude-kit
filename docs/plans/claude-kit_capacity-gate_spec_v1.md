## Goal

A fable-override dispatch under an exhausted weekly allotment discovers the exhaustion by timing out into finishing-work's never-started ladder: the five-minute first-turn window plus the five-minute probe window, then both again for the ladder's same-model retry, roughly twenty minutes per gate, re-paid at every gate in every session for as long as the exhaustion lasts, because the trigger's fact is deliberately per-gate rather than cached. On a machine running claude-swap (the operator's account rotator), the measured answer already sits on disk: claude-swap polls the usage endpoint on its own cadence and writes `~/.claude-swap-backup/cache/usage.json` (per-account five-hour, seven-day, and per-model scoped windows, Fable among them, plus a `last429At` stamp per account) beside `~/.claude-swap-backup/sequence.json` (`activeAccountNumber`). This plan gives dispatch a read-only meter over those two files, so a downgrade is recorded as measured rather than discovered, and the ladder stays live behind the gate as the backstop for everything the meter cannot see.

Four principles, settled in the design dialog and binding on every section:

1. **The reading is good only for the dispatch it was taken for.** claude-swap rebalances the active seat as often as every 60 seconds, so within a minute the machine can go from a nearly exhausted fable window to a fresh one, or the reverse. Both files are read at every fable dispatch, the account identity is never memoized, and nothing shortens the read to once per session or once per plan.
2. **Sessions never poll the usage endpoint.** The endpoint has its own request budget that cumulative polling across surfaces saturates, and a bearer token in a tool call lands in the session transcript in the clear. The meter reads claude-swap's cache and nothing else; where the cache is absent or stale, the answer is no reading, never a fetch.
3. **The reader inherits the broker's posture verbatim.** Plaintext OAuth material sits in the credentials directory beside the cache, and the cache itself carries account emails and organization identifiers. So: exactly two files read, at fixed paths under the home directory, byte-capped, with no content logged or echoed beyond the numeric fields and timestamps the verdict needs, and the reader's output pinned to carry no email, organization identifier, or claim identifier.
4. **The gate is advisory and the ladder is the backstop.** A passing meter does not guarantee the dispatch lands (a swap can move the seat mid-dispatch; the cache's usage figures are up to a poll interval old even when the seat identity is current), so the never-started ladder stays exactly as finishing-work states it, catching transient faults and everything the meter mispredicts.

### 1. The capacity reader. Model: opus

Files in scope: `plugins/claude-kit/hooks/capacity-read.js` (new), `test/capacity-read.test.js` (new), `plugins/claude-kit/hooks/kit-read-lib.js` (read as the boundary, unchanged), `test/size-budget.json` (synced, since `test/` is a measured root and a measured file with no budget entry reds the ratchet).

`plugins/claude-kit/hooks/capacity-read.js`, a CLI with no arguments and no configuration beyond the floor signpost. It reads exactly two fixed paths, `~/.claude-swap-backup/sequence.json` and `~/.claude-swap-backup/cache/usage.json`, each through `readFileBounded` from `kit-read-lib.js` at a 1 MB ceiling, touching nothing else under that directory. A `null` from the boundary (absent, not a regular file, unreadable) and a result whose `bounded` flag is set (the file exceeds the ceiling, or the read ended short) are each a no-reading before any parse is attempted; the reader never parses a partial text. From `sequence.json` it takes `activeAccountNumber` and `lastUpdated`; from `usage.json` the active account's `fetchedAt`, `pollIntervalS`, `last429At`, `backoffUntil`, and `lastGood` windows (`five_hour.pct`, `seven_day.pct`, and the `scoped` entry whose `name` is `Fable`, matched case-insensitively). Nothing throws: an account that does not resolve, or a missing `five_hour`, `seven_day`, or Fable `scoped` window, or a window whose `pct` is not a finite number, is a no-reading with the fixed reason `missing window` (`unknown account` for the account), because a verdict computed over two windows when the dispatch spends three would report headroom it never measured; the context fields `last429At`, `backoffUntil`, and `pollIntervalS` are optional, and their absence changes only the context suffix or the staleness bound the next paragraph states.

The verdict, one line on stdout, in exactly one of three shapes:

- `fable capacity: scoped N%, 7d N%, 5h N% (account <n>, fetched <age>s ago) -> dispatch` where the worst window sits below the floor; where `backoffUntil` is in the future or `last429At` is within the past hour, the line carries that as appended context (`; recent 429` or `; in backoff`) and the verdict stays `-> dispatch`, per the Decisions entry demoting those signals;
- the same reading with `-> downgrade` where the worst of the three windows is at or above the floor;
- `fable capacity: no reading (<reason>) -> ladder governs` where either file is absent, unparseable, over the cap, `activeAccountNumber` resolves to no account, any of the three windows is missing, or the reading is stale, meaning `fetchedAt` older than three times the account's `pollIntervalS` (absent that field, older than 30 minutes) or `sequence.json`'s `lastUpdated` older than 30 minutes, which is the claude-swap-not-running shape. The reason is one of a fixed set the code enumerates in one place: `absent file`, `unreadable`, `over cap`, `unparseable`, `unknown account`, `missing window`, `stale`.

The floor is `capacityGateFloor` in `~/.claude/claude-kit.local.json`, read tolerantly on `compact-deferral-nudge.js`'s signpost pattern, default 100. The output never carries an email, an organization identifier, a claim identifier, a filesystem path, or any string field from either file beyond the fixed vocabulary above and the numeric readings; the no-reading reason is from the fixed set, never an error message quoting file content, and since no path is printed the output needs none of the home-directory elision `docs/security-model.md` reserves to `kit-compact-lib.js`. Exit code 0 for all three shapes (the verdict is the text; a caller branching on exit code would collapse no-reading into one of the others).

Tests red-first, on a redirected-HOME fixture per the suite's house pattern: each verdict shape from a planted cache; the worst-of-three-windows rule (fable low but seven-day at the floor downgrades); the demoted-signal rule both directions (a recent 429 or future backoff below the floor stays `-> dispatch` with the context appended, and at the floor the downgrade names the window, not the 429); each staleness branch; each no-reading branch by its reason (absent directory, absent file, oversized file, unparseable file, unknown account, and each of the three windows missing in turn, every one asserting the `-> ladder governs` shape and its fixed reason); the floor read from the signpost with the default on absence; and the privacy pin, a planted cache whose email and organizationUuid values are distinctive strings asserted absent from stdout and stderr in every verdict shape. The privacy pin's control is the redaction broken deliberately: the reader exposes its output formatter for the test to call with the formatter's redaction bypassed (or the test runs a fixture-local copy of the reader with the redaction removed), and the pin's own assertion is watched to go red on the planted strings before its green over the shipped reader is trusted. Asserting that the planted values appear in the raw file is not the control, since it exercises the fixture and never the assertion.

Acceptance: all three verdict shapes produced from fixtures and watched red first; the privacy pin green with its control spoken as the broken redaction reddening it; no write ever issued under `~/.claude-swap-backup`, asserted as each of the two files' mtime and size and the directory's and the `cache/` subdirectory's entry lists unchanged across a run, in every verdict shape (a directory mtime alone moves only on an entry created, removed, or renamed, so an in-place rewrite would pass it); `test/size-budget.json` synced over the new test file and carried in the section's commit; suite delta against a recorded baseline.

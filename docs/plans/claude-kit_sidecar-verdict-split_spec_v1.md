## Goal

The judgment sidecar judges every completed tool call's result against the intent the caller stated and answers with one of three words: achieved, failed, diverged. The third word covers two things the prompt itself separates with a comma: evidence in the result that contradicts the intent, and an instrument that cannot prove what the intent needs. Read against the fleet's logs, those are different findings with different worth. The route defects, an exit code read off the last command of a pipe, a fixed legend printed under output that contradicts it, an absence read as presence with no control, are the alerts the instrument is uniquely worth keeping for, since a route defect whose answer comes out right leaves no trace anywhere else. The contradictions, a sweep that found three where the caller expected one, are the instrument doing its job on a check that did its job, and under one word they read as false positives and teach a reader to skim the channel that carries the route defects too. When this plan is done: the prompt names four verdicts, `unproven` carrying the instrument-cannot-establish reading and `diverged` keeping the visible contradiction; every surface that reads a verdict carries the fourth word, the verdict record, the findings file, the inbox item, the advisory line a session reads, and the rollup's counts; the sidecar contract states it; and the battery that gates a prompt change measures both the split and the partial-input contracts the current prompt carries without a single case, so a wording change can no longer shift verdicts with no case moving.

## Approach

Split the word on the prompt's own comma. `diverged` keeps the first clause, evidence in the result that contradicts the intent, which is the word's strongest meaning and the one every historical record's `diverged` is a superset of. `unproven` takes the second, the result looks like success but the instrument cannot establish what the intent needs, with the three route-defect shapes the logs show most named in the prompt as its examples. The fan-out set is closed at `diverged` and `unproven`: both reach the findings file and the inbox exactly as `diverged` does today, `achieved` and `failed` land a verdict record and nothing else, an answer outside the prompt's array is `unusable` and lands nothing, and a recognition item is not a verdict and takes the inbox's memory path. Nothing is gated, thresholded or suppressed; what changes is the sentence a session reads, so an `unproven` reads as a warning about the caller's own route and a `diverged` reads as a result to weigh. The prompt ships as `judgment-v5` with v4 frozen, because the prompt id rides every verdict record and verdicts compare only within an id; the battery ships as `judgment-v2` with v1 frozen, for the same reason. The order of the two sections is load-bearing: section 1 mints the prompt and measures it with the battery while the live daemon stays on v4, and section 2 carries the word through every reader and repoints the daemon in the same commit, so no live verdict is ever minted that the pipeline would drop into `other` or fan out nowhere. The daemon loads its code once, so the swap to v5 happens at the restart the Operator Verification names, and until then live verdicts stay v4. The vocabulary stays single-sourced: the battery and the rollup read the prompt module's array rather than re-declaring it, so a fifth word could not land in one surface and not another.

The surfaces that speak the verdict vocabulary were swept by a read-only scout over `sidecar/`, `plugins/claude-kit/hooks/`, `test/`, `docs/` and the archive, searching for the three verdict words, `VERDICTS`, `KNOWN_VERDICTS`, `alertItem`, `formatItem`, `findingRecord` and `promptId`; the surfaces it returned are the ones under Evidence and they are the Files in scope of the two sections, with `docs/backlog.md` in section 1 for the two items that section retires. No other document under `docs/` names the diverged alert: `docs/security-model.md` describes the inbox without naming a verdict and `docs/architecture.md` names none, so neither is in scope.

## Decisions

Decided 2026-09-04 by the Expert seat; reversible at arming.

1. **`diverged` keeps the visible contradiction; `unproven` is the new word for the route defect.** The alternative, giving the new word to the contradiction and keeping `diverged` for the route defect, was weighed and loses: the prompt's own first clause is what the word has always most strongly meant, historical `diverged` records then stay a superset of the new meaning rather than a disjoint set, and `unproven` says what the reader should do, which is establish the claim, where a new word for the contradiction would need to say nothing.
2. **Both words fan out; nothing is suppressed or reweighted.** The NEO coordinator's reading of the logs is that suppressing or marking the divergence-seeking class would have suppressed nearly all of the value, since the fair alerts land inside that class. A label costs nothing the reader was not already paying.
3. **A new prompt id and a new battery version, nothing edited in place.** The contract's comparability rule, and the same reason v3 and v4 were minted rather than edited.
4. **The tool-side-cut case is pinned and measured, not necessarily repaired.** The backlog's own instruction is to pin the case before any wording moves; the acceptance is no regression on the false-achieved traps the v3 prompt exists to fix. The case's acceptable set is hand-adjudicated from its text before any run, per the batteries README's opening rule, and v5's verdict on it is recorded beside that set whatever it is; a miss counts against the floor and the set is never widened to absorb it.
5. **A dispatch's launch receipt is adjudicated achieved, against an intent that states the launch.** An asynchronous dispatch's result is a receipt that the launch happened; that is the whole of what the call could show. The case's intent is therefore the launch itself, that the run was started in the background and a handle returned, which the receipt meets, so the case accepts achieved and a prompt that reads it as unproven or diverged fails the case. An intent stated as the dispatched work's outcome is a different triple, one the receipt cannot establish and v5's own `unproven` sentence covers, and this case does not carry it.
6. **The battery measures v5 before the daemon runs it.** Section 1 repoints only the battery; section 2 repoints the judge and the daemon in the commit that teaches every reader the fourth word. The alternative, landing the prompt first, would have the live daemon answering `unproven` for the route-defect class while the daemon fans out only `diverged` and the rollup buckets the word as `other`, losing exactly the alerts this plan exists to keep, for as long as section 2 took.

### 2. The pipeline carries `unproven` from the verdict record to the reader. Model: opus

Point `sidecar/judge.js` and `sidecar/daemon.js` at v5 in this section's commit, so the live daemon's next restart runs the prompt this pipeline carries. In `sidecar/logs.js`, `findingRecord` carries the verdict word in a `verdict` field, its `type` carries that same word (`diverged` or `unproven`, never a fixed literal, so a durable finding's type cannot contradict its verdict), and it carries `truncated` as the verdict record does, so a finding can be read for what its input was. In `plugins/claude-kit/hooks/kit-sidecar-capture.js`, `formatItem` composes two sentences by verdict: for `unproven`, `verdict alert (call <id>): stated intent "<intent>" unproven; the check did not establish it; sidecar reason "<reason>". Weigh the result, not the instrument.`; an item carrying no verdict field renders today's sentence unchanged, which is the shape every item already queued has; an item whose `verdict` is neither fan-out word is skipped exactly as an unknown kind is, since the inbox is an ordinary file and the hook trusts no writer's fields. In `sidecar/CONTRACT.md`, the `promptId` paragraph names `judgment-v5`, the inbox `kind` row says an alert carries a diverged or an unproven verdict and names the new field, the judge-is-told bullet names the word the absence leniency now bears on, and one sentence states the four words and which two fan out. `docs/security-model.md` and `docs/architecture.md` name no verdict and no diverged alert, and a verdict word changes no egress, so neither document is touched.

## Goal

The executing-work skill's findings step says what to do with a Critical, a Major and a Minor, and says when a fix delta owes another review round, and never says when the loop ends. Every run derives its own stop, and the derivation that comes naturally, a round returning only Minors closes the loop, cannot terminate on a section whose remaining findings are claims: a comment, a header sentence, a test's because-string, a test instrument's stated reach. A max-effort reviewer can always find a sentence that claims one word more than the code proves, every fix writes new sentences, and the charters rate a false sentence Major under maintainability damage, so the loop runs until someone stops it by hand. The kit has recorded this shape four times without acting on it: a fifteen-round section with two consults on this machine (2026-09-03), a thirteen-round seesaw and a finishing pass with no stopping rule (2026-09-07, this machine), and a seventeen-round section over thirty hours on NEO-CLAUDE (2026-09-07), plus the operator-tier memory that states the structural reason and the journal entry that says a count-keyed rule misfires. When this plan is done: executing-work's findings step owns one class definition, telling a behavior finding from a claim finding by whether it states a failure scenario, and one terminal condition keyed on it; the code-reviewer charters carry that definition as a pinned copy and rate a claim finding Minor under a `[claim]` tag, with two narrowly drawn exceptions that keep their bar; the recurrence rule sends a claims class to a mechanical check or a deletion rather than a Standing Brief Amendment; a behavior Major that reverses the prior round's fix on the same passage mandates a consult at the second reversal; responding-to-review states that a claim finding's fix is a deletion or a check and never a rewritten sentence; the finishing pass points at the same condition for the fix rounds it runs; and a scenario probe holds the moment so a later wording change that reopens the loop is a finding rather than a surprise.

## Approach

Key the exit on the class of a finding rather than on its count or its rating. The class definition is three sentences executing-work owns. A behavior finding states a failure scenario, an input or a state on which the code does the wrong thing on a reachable path or a test exercises the wrong thing, so its fix changes what runs. A claim finding states none, and its fix changes a sentence and nothing that runs: a comment, a header, a docstring, a test's because-string or title, a test instrument's stated reach. Two exceptions keep a claim finding's bar: a claim on a security boundary, meaning any of the surfaces step 3's security-reviewer trigger names (input handling, authentication or authorization, SQL construction, secrets or configuration, shell or process execution, an external boundary), where the security reviewer's judgment stands; and a claim on a published contract surface, a README, a skill, a charter, a document under `docs/`, where a false sentence inside the section's own delta that contradicts an acceptance criterion or a principle the plan states is owed like a behavior finding, and any other claim there rates as a claim finding does. That second exception is drawn narrowly on purpose: in the kit's own repository nearly every section edits a skill or a charter, so an exception that kept every sentence on those surfaces at full bar would keep the loop open on exactly the sections this plan exists to close, which is what the 2026-09-03 inbox note's derived condition (delta-internal, contradicts an acceptance clause or a principle) already answered. The class is read at adjudication, before any fix exists, from whether the finding states a failure scenario; the fix then follows the class, and where a finding could be closed either way the orchestrator's choice is recorded in the Chapter, a sentence-fix chosen over a behavior-fix on a finding that stated a failure scenario being a Major justified-not-fixed rather than a claim dispositioned. The terminal condition follows: a section's review loop ends at the first round over the latest delta that surfaces no Critical and no owed Major, an owed Major being a behavior finding or a published-surface claim of the kind the second exception names. Claim findings from that round are dispositioned in the same fix round by deletion of the false sentence, or by a mechanical check where one is cheap and the claim is worth keeping, else recorded in the Chapter as a file-audit line; a fix delta made only of claim fixes owes no round of its own unless it trips one of the three existing triggers. Severity follows the class in the charters, so the reviewers stop rating a false sentence Major in the ordinary case, and the terminal condition still keys on the class rather than the rating, so a reviewer that ignores the tag cannot reopen the loop. The recurrence rule splits on the same class: a second instance of a behavior class still writes a Standing Brief Amendment; a second instance of a claims class writes a mechanical check or runs a deletion sweep and never an amendment, with the memory record's reason carried into the skill in one sentence, since a rule that gives no reason is the one a later reader relaxes. The one loop the terminal condition does not bound, an owed Major that reverses the prior round's fix on the same passage, is bounded by naming it an instance of the consult skill's trigger (a) at the second reversal on one passage (round N+2 asking to undo what round N+1 did to round N's fix, on the same lines), whose ruling closes the passage; no numeric cap is added, per Decision 3.

The definition lives in executing-work inside a marked region, because agents inherit no skills and so the charters cannot point at it: the adversarial and blind charters carry the region as a designed byte-identical copy, and a parity test pins the set, the same shape the output style's register core takes against the doctrine. responding-to-review and finishing-work point at executing-work rather than copying, since both are skills a session loads. The probe is authored `proposed`, with its answer awaiting the operator's ruling in the rulings batch, so its after leg is evidence and the RED and GREEN reps stand for section 1 as writing-skills states.

## Decisions

Decided 2026-09-07 by the operator at the keyboard of the KIT: Expert session, on the Expert's recommendation.

1. **The exit keys on the class of a finding, never on the reviewer's count or rating.** A count-keyed rule terminates only where the class shrinks with each fix, and a claims class produces new reviewable surface with every fix. The class is decided by what the fix changes, which the orchestrator can read off the fix itself, so the rule holds even against a reviewer that rates every sentence Major.
2. **A claim finding rates Minor under a `[claim]` tag, with two exceptions that keep their bar.** The alternative, leaving the charters unchanged and having only the terminal condition ignore claim Majors, was weighed and loses: it fills the Chapter with justified-not-fixed Majors and puts the whole classification burden on adjudication every round. The exceptions are a claim on a security boundary, keyed on the security-reviewer trigger surfaces so the charters have a predicate rather than a word, and a claim on a published contract surface that sits inside the section's delta and contradicts an acceptance criterion or a plan principle; the second is drawn that narrowly because a file-list exception (every skill, every charter) would keep the loop open on the kit's own prose sections, which are the local instances that surfaced this.
3. **A consult at the second reversal bounds the seesaw; no numeric round cap.** A numeric cap would close a code section with a real behavior Major standing at round N, and the consult skill already exists for a second failed attempt at the same problem. The seesaw is named as an instance of that trigger rather than as a new one, so the consult skill's own text changes by one clause at most.
4. **The definition is owned by executing-work and copied into the two code-reviewer charters under a parity pin.** An agent charter cannot resolve a pointer into a skill, so the copy is unavoidable; pinning it byte-identical is what keeps the copy from becoming a second definition.
5. **A claim finding's fix is a deletion or a mechanical check, never a rewritten sentence.** A rewritten sentence re-enters the class it was written to leave. Where the claim is worth keeping and a check is cheap, the check is the fix; where neither holds, the sentence goes.
6. **Standing Brief Amendments stay the remedy for a behavior class and stop being one for a claims class.** The memory record measured the amendment failing on a claims class twice, once in the module it was adopted for, and the NEO run reproduced it at round 10.
7. **The security reviewer's charter is untouched.** Its findings on a boundary are the first exception by construction, and its closing sentence already asks for severity honesty in both directions.
